FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Is it free?

Yes. Finding your weekend is free, and so is the full itinerary once you join the list. We may offer paid extras one day, but never before telling you.

Are the prices up to date?

Not live yet. For now we use realistic estimates for a round trip from Paris, booked a few weeks ahead. That's why we say “prices are estimates”. Live prices are on our roadmap.

How do you make money?

We don't, yet. Later, some booking links may become affiliate links: if you book through them we'd earn a small commission and you'd pay the same price. We'll always label them clearly.

What do you do with my email?

We use it to send your itinerary and one follow-up, plus cheap ticket alerts only if you tick that box. One click to unsubscribe, and we never sell your data.

Is this real AI?

Yes, used where it helps. Your match comes from clear rules on our curated data, so the maths is always right. Then an AI model (Google Gemini) writes your email from your answers, and an automation in n8n sorts new sign-ups for the team. The prices themselves are estimates, not live prices.
